How to use

  1. One-way, tab: open a tab playing audio, click Start, share that tab with tab audio enabled.
  2. One-way, microphone: choose Microphone, allow mic access, play phone or app audio near the computer mic.
  3. Two-way, LINE Web: choose Chrome tab as inbound source and pick the call tab.
  4. Two-way, LINE app: set LINE speaker/output to BlackHole 16ch, then choose that device as Their voice source.
  5. For two-way always pick BlackHole 2ch as outbound, and your real headphones/speakers as inbound output.
